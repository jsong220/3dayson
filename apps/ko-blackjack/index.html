Basic Strategy

Quick-reference rules. “Double” means hit if doubling is unavailable.

Splitting

  • Always split: Aces and 8s
  • Never split: 10s and 5s
  • Split 2s, 3s, and 7s: Against dealer 2 through 7
  • Split 4s: Against dealer 5 and 6
  • Split 6s: Against dealer 2 through 6
  • Split 9s: Against dealer 2 through 9, except 7

Soft Totals

  • Soft 21 and 20: Always stand
  • Soft 19 (A, 8): Double against 6; otherwise stand
  • Soft 18 (A, 7): Double against 2–6, stand against 7 and 8, hit against 9, 10, and Ace. If you cannot double against 2–6, stand.
  • Soft 17 (A, 6): Double against 3–6; otherwise hit
  • Soft 16 and 15 (A, 5 and A, 4): Double against 4–6; otherwise hit
  • Soft 14 and 13 (A, 3 and A, 2): Double against 5 and 6; otherwise hit

Hard Totals & Surrender

  • Surrender 16: Against dealer 9 through Ace
  • Surrender 15: Against dealer 10
  • Hard 17 and above: Always stand
  • Hard 13 through 16: Stand against 2–6; hit against 7–Ace
  • Hard 12: Stand against 4–6; hit against everything else
  • Hard 11: Always double down
  • Hard 10: Double against 2–9
  • Hard 9: Double against 3–6
  • Hard 8 and below: Always hit

reKO Strategy Indexes

When index training is enabled in settings, the app tests you on these reKO plays (rules from qfit.com/rekostrategy.htm). reKO uses one index for every play: the running count of +2. Below +2 play basic strategy. At +2 and above play the hands below differently. Everything not listed follows basic strategy.
